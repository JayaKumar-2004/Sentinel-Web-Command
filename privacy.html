1. Academic Project Status

Raksha-Suraksha is a proprietary academic project engineered to demonstrate autonomous SOS and Context-Aware Auto-Responder capabilities. We adhere strictly to a "Privacy by Design" framework.

2. Tier 1: User Authentication

  • Managed securely via Google Firebase. We log only your email address to generate a unique cryptographic UID.

3. Tier 2: Local Device Data

  • Location Data (GPS): Accessed only during an active SOS trigger.
  • Telephony logs: Scanned locally to manage the Auto-Responder.

4. Tier 3: Encrypted Cloud Evidence Vault

  • Evidence is uploaded directly to a private Cloudinary Media Vault in the sentinel_evidence directory.

5. TRAI COMPLIANCE

Engineered in strict accordance with the Telecom Regulatory Authority of India (TRAI) guidelines.
