1. Academic Project Status

Raksha-Suraksha is a proprietary academic project engineered to demonstrate autonomous SOS and Context-Aware Auto-Responder capabilities. We adhere strictly to a "Privacy by Design" framework.

2. Tier 1: User Authentication

To access the Secure Evidence Portal, users must verify their identity:

  • Gmail OAuth: Managed securely via Google Firebase. We log only your email address to generate a unique cryptographic UID.
  • We do not share, sell, or use your email for marketing purposes.

3. Tier 2: Local Device Data (SOS & Responder)

Raksha-Suraksha requests access to sensitive hardware solely for autonomous protection:

  • Location Data (GPS): Accessed only during an active SOS trigger. Coordinates are dispatched locally via SMS to trusted contacts and are never stored on our external servers.
  • Telephony & SMS Logs: Scanned locally on the device to manage the Context-Aware Auto-Responder. These logs never leave your device.
  • Camera & Microphone: Accessed exclusively when you manually trigger the Secure Evidence Vault.

4. Tier 3: Encrypted Cloud Evidence Vault

Video evidence captured in the vault is handled with strict security protocols:

  • Evidence is uploaded directly to a private Cloudinary Media Vault.
  • Files are isolated in the sentinel_evidence directory.
  • Every log is permanently linked to your verified Firebase UID to ensure a secure chain of custody.

5. Data Sovereignty

As the user, you retain absolute ownership of your data. You may revoke camera, microphone, and location permissions at any time via your Android device settings.

6. TRAI & FEDERAL TELECOM COMPLIANCE

Raksha-Suraksha is engineered in strict accordance with the Telecom Regulatory Authority of India (TRAI) guidelines for autonomous communications:

  • P2P Routing Exemption: SOS exfiltration utilizes native device-level Person-to-Person (P2P) SMS routing to trusted whitelisted contacts, exempting the system from commercial DLT (Distributed Ledger Technology) blockchain registration.
  • Anti-Spam & Fair Usage: The Context-Aware Auto-Responder operates strictly within the TRAI mandate of 100 SMS per day, utilizing high-value, low-frequency telemetry bursts to prevent network-level spam blocks.
  • ERSS Alignment: Software protocols complement the Ministry of Home Affairs' Rule 112 (Emergency Response Support System) mandate for mobile hardware panic buttons.
