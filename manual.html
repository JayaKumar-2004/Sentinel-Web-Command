Tactical Manual

Complete operational guide for deploying and commanding the Raksha-Suraksha system.

01

App Deployment & Permissions

Step A: Install the Sentinel-X APK

Download the official Raksha-Suraksha APK to your target Android device. Since it is a system-level security app, you must allow installation from "Unknown Sources" in your Android settings.

Step B: Grant Device Administrator

Upon opening the app, you will be prompted to activate Device Administrator rights. This is a critical security measure that prevents a thief from uninstalling the app or force-stopping it from the settings menu.

Step C: Enable Accessibility & Location

The app requires Accessibility Services to remotely lock the screen and force volume overrides. It also requires Location (Allow all the time) to transmit live GPS coordinates to the dashboard.

⚠️ MANDATORY DND OVERRIDE

You must allow the app to override "Do Not Disturb" settings so that the Anti-Theft Siren can ring at 100% volume even if the phone is on silent mode.

02

Authorizing the Uplink

Step A: Create an Agent Account

On this website, navigate to the Web Terminal and click "Secure Login" to register a new account. Use a secure email and a strong master passcode.

Step B: Login on the Android App

Open the Sentinel-X app on your phone and log in using the exact same email and passcode you used on the website. This securely binds your physical phone to your web dashboard.

Step C: Verify the Connection

Open the Device Linking module on your Web Dashboard. You should instantly see your phone's hardware model (e.g., "Samsung Galaxy") listed as [ONLINE].

03

Web Terminal Operations (Online)

If your device is connected to the internet (WiFi or Mobile Data), you can control it instantly from the Web Terminal's "App Controls" module.

Trigger Anti-Theft Siren

Clicking TRIGGER SIREN will bypass the phone's silent mode, force the media volume to 100%, and play an inescapable high-pitch alarm.

Instant Screen Lock

Clicking INSTANT LOCK utilizes the Accessibility Service to instantly turn off the screen and lock the device, protecting your personal data from thieves.

Force Emergency Dial

Using the Custom SOS Auto-Dialer, you can type a number into the dashboard, and the phone will autonomously open its dialer and call that number (e.g., 112 for Police).

04

Offline SMS Fallback

If a thief turns off your mobile data, the web terminal cannot reach the phone. You must use any other phone to send specific text messages (SMS) to your stolen phone's number to trigger offline actions.

Trigger Siren (Offline)

Send the following exact text message to your stolen phone to force the alarm to sound:

RAKSHA_ALARM_ON

Lock Screen (Offline)

Send the following text message to instantly lock the display:

RAKSHA_LOCK_NOW

Get GPS Location (Offline)

Send the following text message to force the phone to reply with a Google Maps link of its current location:

RAKSHA_LOCATE_ME
