Regulatory Framework

Raksha-Suraksha is engineered in strict accordance with the guidelines set by the Telecom Regulatory Authority of India (TRAI) and the Department of Telecommunications (DoT).

⬅️ RETURN TO DASHBOARD

1. P2P SMS Routing

SOS exfiltration utilizes native device-level SMS routing directly from the user's SIM, classifying it as P2P communication.

2. DLT Registration Exemption

The system is exempt from DLT blockchain registration for commercial telemarketers.

3. Anti-Spam Policy

Operates strictly within the TRAI mandate of 100 SMS per day per SIM.

4. ERSS (Rule 112) Alignment

Designed to complement the Ministry of Home Affairs' Rule 112 regarding physical hardware panic buttons.

5. NCPR / DND Override

Emergency SMS alerts bypass Do Not Disturb (DND) registry blocks.

6. Zero Interception Architecture

Does not intercept or block standard telecom routing.
