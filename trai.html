Federal Telecom & Emergency System Protocols

Regulatory Framework

Raksha-Suraksha adheres to the following guidelines set by the Telecom Regulatory Authority of India (TRAI) and the Department of Telecommunications (DoT).

1. P2P (Person-to-Person) SMS Routing

SOS exfiltration utilizes native device-level SMS routing directly from the user's SIM to whitelisted contacts, classifying it as P2P communication rather than A2P (Application-to-Person).

2. DLT Registration Exemption

Because the architecture avoids centralized 3rd-party bulk SMS servers (e.g., Twilio), the system is exempt from Distributed Ledger Technology (DLT) blockchain registration for commercial telemarketers.

3. Anti-Spam & Fair Usage Policy

The Context-Aware Auto-Responder operates strictly within the TRAI mandate of 100 SMS per day per SIM. High-value telemetry bursts ensure the network never flags the device for spam.

4. ERSS (Rule 112) Alignment

The software protocols are designed to complement the Ministry of Home Affairs' Rule 112 (Emergency Response Support System) mandate regarding physical hardware panic buttons on Indian mobile devices.

5. NCPR / DND Override for Emergencies

Emergency SMS alerts sent from a personal number to explicitly whitelisted contacts do not fall under Unsolicited Commercial Communications (UCC), bypassing Do Not Disturb (DND) registry blocks.

6. Explicit Opt-In Telemetry

Users must explicitly grant SMS, Call Log, and Background Location permissions at the OS level before the autonomous guardian system can interface with the telecom network.

7. Localized Data Parsing

Telephony and SMS logs are parsed locally on the device to determine threat contexts (e.g., fake switch-off detection). This raw telecom data is never exfiltrated to external servers.

8. Data Localization Mandates

All Tier-3 cloud operations and Firebase media evidence uploads are processed securely, ensuring alignment with Indian data localization preferences for sensitive telemetry.

9. Misuse Prevention Protocols

The system features strict cooldown timers on automated SOS dispatch to prevent accidental network flooding or denial-of-service (DoS) conditions on local cell towers.

10. Transparent User Revocation

Users maintain complete data sovereignty and can immediately revoke the app's ability to interface with the telecom network via standard Android permission settings.

11. Carrier Agnostic Operation

The system operates independently of specific Telecom Service Providers (TSPs) like Jio, Airtel, or Vi, ensuring universal functionality across all Indian telecom networks.

12. Zero Interception Architecture

Raksha-Suraksha does not intercept, modify, or block incoming calls or messages intended for the user, maintaining the integrity of standard telecom routing.
